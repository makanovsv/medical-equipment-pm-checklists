# Medical equipment preventive maintenance checklists

Free, vendor-neutral preventive maintenance (PM) checklists for 10 types of medical equipment, with a CSV record sheet for each visit. English and Russian.

Published by [Service Engineer Academy](https://serviceengineer.academy/), an online school for medical equipment service engineers. The items are the checklists taught in our Level 1 courses. Web versions with explanations: https://serviceengineer.academy/guides/preventive-maintenance-checklists/

**Use them with the manufacturer's service manual, not instead of it.** The manual sets intervals, kit parts, test limits and calibration steps for your exact model. Where the manual is stricter, the manual wins.
