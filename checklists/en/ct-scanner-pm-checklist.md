
# CT scanner preventive maintenance checklist

This is the checklist we use in the course [CT Service — Level 1](https://serviceengineer.academy/course/ct-l1/). It lists what to check on a CT scanner during planned maintenance, in the order we teach it. Print it or download the CSV and fill in one sheet per visit.

**Use it with the manufacturer's service manual, not instead of it.** The manual sets the intervals, kit parts, test limits and calibration steps for your exact model and software. Where the manual is stricter, the manual wins.

## Checklist

1. ☐ Interview operators; read error log, tube counters, quality log; check disk space, archive and backup
2. ☐ Listen to the gantry at speed before shutdown; measure room temperature and humidity
3. ☐ Confirm no patients are booked and the clinic has a backup plan
4. ☐ Shut down by procedure, isolate, lock and tag, verify absence of voltage, wait the discharge time
5. ☐ Engage the rotor lock before any work inside the rotating part
6. ☐ Record as-found condition with photographs before cleaning
7. ☐ Slip ring: brushes against wear criterion, ring tracks, carbon dust removed by vacuum and approved materials; no compressed air, no lubricant
8. ☐ Data link cleaned by approved method; belt and bearing checked; nothing loose on the rotor
9. ☐ Table: movement, play, travel accuracy, emergency release, pedals, controls
10. ☐ Lasers aligned to scan plane and isocentre per manufacturer method; offset recorded
11. ☐ Cables and connectors inspected; HV cables only externally
12. ☐ Every emergency stop, door interlock and warning light tested
13. ☐ Warm-up, air calibration, phantom check and test images before release; results recorded and compared

Download the record sheet: [CSV](https://serviceengineer.academy/guides/ct-scanner-pm-checklist.csv). It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.

## Record on every sheet

- Asset number, serial number, model, software version and location
- Date, engineer, and the analyser or test tools used with their calibration dates
- For each step: pass, fail or not applicable; the measured value and the limit where there is one
- Parts replaced, with part numbers
- Decision: in service, in service with restriction, or out of service; next due date
