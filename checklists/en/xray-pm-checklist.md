
# X-ray system preventive maintenance checklist

This is the checklist we use in the course [X-ray Service — Level 1](https://serviceengineer.academy/course/xray-l1/). It lists what to check on an X-ray system during planned maintenance, in the order we teach it. Print it or download the CSV and fill in one sheet per visit.

**Use it with the manufacturer's service manual, not instead of it.** The manual sets the intervals, kit parts, test limits and calibration steps for your exact model and software. Where the manual is stricter, the manual wins.

## Checklist

1. ☐ Prepare the test sheet with the limits for this model before the visit
2. ☐ Check meter and safety analyser calibration dates
3. ☐ Make as-found measurements before cleaning or adjusting
4. ☐ Test every safety function, including warning lights and emergency stops
5. ☐ Measure kV, time, reproducibility, linearity, output and HVL; fluoroscopy dose rates and timer where relevant
6. ☐ Check light field against X-ray field, centring, distance indicator, automatic collimation, AEC
7. ☐ Run phantom images with the baseline setup; save images and settings
8. ☐ Finish with IEC 62353 electrical safety testing and a report with a clear fitness-for-use statement

Download the record sheet: [CSV](https://serviceengineer.academy/guides/xray-pm-checklist.csv). It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.

## Record on every sheet

- Asset number, serial number, model, software version and location
- Date, engineer, and the analyser or test tools used with their calibration dates
- For each step: pass, fail or not applicable; the measured value and the limit where there is one
- Parts replaced, with part numbers
- Decision: in service, in service with restriction, or out of service; next due date
