
# Ultrasound system preventive maintenance checklist

This is the checklist we use in the course [Ultrasound Service — Level 1](https://serviceengineer.academy/course/us-l1/). It lists what to check on an ultrasound system during planned maintenance, in the order we teach it. Print it or download the CSV and fill in one sheet per visit.

**Use it with the manufacturer's service manual, not instead of it.** The manual sets the intervals, kit parts, test limits and calibration steps for your exact model and software. Where the manual is stricter, the manual wins.

## Checklist

### System

1. ☐ Agree the time with the department and collect the users' list of complaints
2. ☐ Record serial number, software version, probe list and on-screen errors; export logs before changing anything
3. ☐ Quick image check on every probe before maintenance
4. ☐ Proper software shutdown, then mains disconnection before cleaning
5. ☐ Clean or replace every air filter; confirm every fan runs quietly; record temperatures if available
6. ☐ Inspect and clean probe ports; test recognition of every probe in every working port
7. ☐ Check the cart: wheels, brakes, lifting column, monitor arm, cable hooks, probe holders, mains cord
8. ☐ Clean the control panel with approved agents; clean and test trackball, keys, encoders, TGC sliders, touch screen
9. ☐ Check monitor, printers and other peripherals the department uses
10. ☐ Check UPS status and age, internal batteries for swelling and run time, date and time
11. ☐ Check free disk space; back up presets and configuration to hospital-controlled media and record where
12. ☐ Send a test image to PACS and query the worklist
13. ☐ No software updates, options or licence changes without authorisation
14. ☐ Electrical safety (lesson 5), image quality (lesson 7), report (lesson 8)

### Probe inspection

15. ☐ Confirm the probe has been reprocessed; wear gloves; record model and serial number first
16. ☐ Ask what was seen, in which preset, since when, after what event, and on which ports
17. ☐ Inspect in a fixed order: lens, housing, strain reliefs, cable, connector, system port; photograph findings
18. ☐ Run the air image test, flex the cable at both ends, and repeat on a second port
19. ☐ Locate gaps with a moist finger or a smooth wire; remember phased arrays may hide dead elements
20. ☐ Compare with the phantom baseline for that probe, and use a probe tester where available
21. ☐ Record one decision — in service, watch, repair or withdraw — tag the probe and inform the department in writing

Download the record sheet: [CSV](https://serviceengineer.academy/guides/ultrasound-pm-checklist.csv). It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.

## Record on every sheet

- Asset number, serial number, model, software version and location
- Date, engineer, and the analyser or test tools used with their calibration dates
- For each step: pass, fail or not applicable; the measured value and the limit where there is one
- Parts replaced, with part numbers
- Decision: in service, in service with restriction, or out of service; next due date
