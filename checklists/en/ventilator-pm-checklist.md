
# Ventilator preventive maintenance checklist

This is the checklist we use in the course [Ventilator Service — Level 1](https://serviceengineer.academy/course/vent-l1/). It lists what to check on a ventilator during planned maintenance, in the order we teach it. Print it or download the CSV and fill in one sheet per visit.

**Use it with the manufacturer's service manual, not instead of it.** The manual sets the intervals, kit parts, test limits and calibration steps for your exact model and software. Where the manual is stricter, the manual wins.

## Checklist

1. ☐ Register entry exists: model, serial, revision and options, software, location, last PM date and hours, next due date and hours
2. ☐ Kit part number confirmed for this serial number and options (with or without turbine / compressor)
3. ☐ Kit contents checked against the list on arrival; O2 cell and battery dates checked
4. ☐ Machine out of use, tagged, decontaminated; time agreed with the department for the full procedure
5. ☐ Hour counters, software version and error log recorded before work
6. ☐ General inspection done; defects outside the kit recorded
7. ☐ Gas isolated and depressurised; kit fitted per procedure, no unapproved lubricant
8. ☐ Separate-schedule items replaced if due (O2 cell, batteries, coin cell, HEPA, compressor kit)
9. ☐ Required calibrations done on a verified gas supply
10. ☐ Full checkout passed; analyzer verification, alarms, battery switch-over and electrical safety done
11. ☐ PM reminder reset only after the work; report written with part numbers, lots, measured values and analyzer details; next due date set

Download the record sheet: [CSV](https://serviceengineer.academy/guides/ventilator-pm-checklist.csv). It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.

## Record on every sheet

- Asset number, serial number, model, software version and location
- Date, engineer, and the analyser or test tools used with their calibration dates
- For each step: pass, fail or not applicable; the measured value and the limit where there is one
- Parts replaced, with part numbers
- Decision: in service, in service with restriction, or out of service; next due date
