
# Patient monitor preventive maintenance checklist

This is the checklist we use in the course [Patient Monitor Service — Level 1](https://serviceengineer.academy/course/mon-l1/). It lists what to check on a patient monitor during planned maintenance, in the order we teach it. Print it or download the CSV and fill in one sheet per visit.

**Use it with the manufacturer's service manual, not instead of it.** The manual sets the intervals, kit parts, test limits and calibration steps for your exact model and software. Where the manual is stricter, the manual wins.

## Checklist

1. ☐ Monitor free of patient; cleaned with an agent the manufacturer allows
2. ☐ Asset number, serial, software version and fitted modules recorded
3. ☐ Hospital configuration exported or photographed before any change
4. ☐ Housing, display, mains cord, patient connectors, labels and mounting inspected
5. ☐ Every accessory flexed and inspected; damaged or non-compatible ones replaced or removed
6. ☐ Power-on self-test, start-up tone, keys, knob and touch screen checked
7. ☐ Each alarm priority provoked, seen and heard at the bedside and at the central station
8. ☐ Alarm volume range, alarm pause time-out and nurse call output checked
9. ☐ Mains-to-battery switch-over without restart; battery status or run time recorded against the manufacturer's criterion
10. ☐ Error log read and relevant entries recorded before any clearing
11. ☐ Date and time correct or synchronised; correction noted
12. ☐ Central station shows correct bed, waveforms and alarms
13. ☐ Configuration compared with the saved record and restored

Download the record sheet: [CSV](https://serviceengineer.academy/guides/patient-monitor-pm-checklist.csv). It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.

## Record on every sheet

- Asset number, serial number, model, software version and location
- Date, engineer, and the analyser or test tools used with their calibration dates
- For each step: pass, fail or not applicable; the measured value and the limit where there is one
- Parts replaced, with part numbers
- Decision: in service, in service with restriction, or out of service; next due date
