
# Infusion and syringe pump preventive maintenance checklist

This is the checklist we use in the course [Infusion and Syringe Pump Service — Level 1](https://serviceengineer.academy/course/infusion-l1/). It lists what to check on an infusion and syringe pump during planned maintenance, in the order we teach it. Print it or download the CSV and fill in one sheet per visit.

**Use it with the manufacturer's service manual, not instead of it.** The manual sets the intervals, kit parts, test limits and calibration steps for your exact model and software. Where the manual is stricter, the manual wins.

## Checklist

1. ☐ Use the manufacturer's battery test and record the measured runtime or condition
2. ☐ Replace swollen, leaking or failing packs with the specified type only
3. ☐ Follow the manufacturer's steps after pack replacement and recheck battery alarms
4. ☐ Check the mains-failure alarm by pulling mains during a run
5. ☐ Inspect for fluid residue, corrosion and cracks at every PM
6. ☐ Compare the ward's disinfectant with the manufacturer's compatible list
7. ☐ Clear the maintenance reminder only after the PM is complete, by the manufacturer's procedure
8. ☐ Record values, instruments, parts and the next due date

Download the record sheet: [CSV](https://serviceengineer.academy/guides/infusion-pump-pm-checklist.csv). It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.

## Record on every sheet

- Asset number, serial number, model, software version and location
- Date, engineer, and the analyser or test tools used with their calibration dates
- For each step: pass, fail or not applicable; the measured value and the limit where there is one
- Parts replaced, with part numbers
- Decision: in service, in service with restriction, or out of service; next due date
