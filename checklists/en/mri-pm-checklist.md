
# MRI system preventive maintenance checklist

This is the checklist we use in the course [MRI Service — Level 1](https://serviceengineer.academy/course/mri-l1/). It lists what to check on an MRI system during planned maintenance, in the order we teach it. Print it or download the CSV and fill in one sheet per visit.

**Use it with the manufacturer's service manual, not instead of it.** The manual sets the intervals, kit parts, test limits and calibration steps for your exact model and software. Where the manual is stricter, the manual wins.

## Checklist

1. ☐ Before entry: previous report, equipment file, checklist; pockets emptied, self-screened; only marked non-magnetic tools
2. ☐ Safety: 5 gauss line marking, signage, oxygen monitor status, quench pipe outlet (if accessible), emergency buttons labelled, no ferromagnetic items in the room
3. ☐ Staff interview: changes since last visit, artefact complaints, interrupted exams, noise changes, status of previous recommendations
4. ☐ Cryogenics: helium level, magnet pressure, compressor pressures, cold head hours, last cold head and adsorber service dates, frost or condensation, cold head sound
5. ☐ Event log: exported, compared with last visit, recurring and first-in-cascade errors noted
6. ☐ Room and infrastructure: lesson 5 checklist completed
7. ☐ Compressor: air filter or condenser cleaned / water strainer and flow checked per manufacturer
8. ☐ Cooling cabinet and chiller: readings recorded, filters checked, no leaks
9. ☐ Magnet covers: intact, fastened, no liquid traces, no rattles
10. ☐ Patient environment: call bulb, two-way intercom, headphones, bore light, bore fan, bore clear and undamaged
11. ☐ Table: full travel in all axes, smooth and quiet, position indication correct, manual release works freely
12. ☐ Tabletop, built-in coil connectors, cushions, straps; detachable table or trolley docking, brakes and MR labelling
13. ☐ Every coil by type and serial number: housing, cable, connector, latches, recognised by system, coil test or phantom scan passed
14. ☐ Any coil with heat marks, cracks or unreliable recognition taken out of use, labelled, reported in writing
15. ☐ Manufacturer's system tests and image quality test with the standard phantom, values recorded
16. ☐ Configuration and calibration backup done or date verified; free disk space recorded; PACS transfer working
17. ☐ Close-out: no active errors, test scan, room clean, site logbook entry, report with findings and owners, verbal handover to person in charge

Download the record sheet: [CSV](https://serviceengineer.academy/guides/mri-pm-checklist.csv). It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.

## Record on every sheet

- Asset number, serial number, model, software version and location
- Date, engineer, and the analyser or test tools used with their calibration dates
- For each step: pass, fail or not applicable; the measured value and the limit where there is one
- Parts replaced, with part numbers
- Decision: in service, in service with restriction, or out of service; next due date
