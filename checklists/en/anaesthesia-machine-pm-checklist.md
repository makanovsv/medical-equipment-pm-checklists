
# Anaesthesia machine preventive maintenance checklist

This is the checklist we use in the course [Anaesthesia Workstation Service — Level 1](https://serviceengineer.academy/course/anes-l1/). It lists what to check on an anaesthesia machine during planned maintenance, in the order we teach it. Print it or download the CSV and fill in one sheet per visit.

**Use it with the manufacturer's service manual, not instead of it.** The manual sets the intervals, kit parts, test limits and calibration steps for your exact model and software. Where the manual is stricter, the manual wins.

## Checklist

1. ☐ PM chapter for the exact model and software found; kit contents and intervals checked
2. ☐ Last PM report, checkout log and open faults reviewed
3. ☐ Machine out of clinical use and tagged; staff informed
4. ☐ Breathing system decontaminated per hospital procedure; PPE used
5. ☐ Vaporisers removed and kept upright; supplies disconnected and pressure vented
6. ☐ Vaporiser port o-rings, absorber and breathing-system seals replaced per kit
7. ☐ Only the specified oxygen-compatible lubricant used, only where specified
8. ☐ Vaporiser interlock checked in every position combination
9. ☐ Flow sensors fitted in correct ports and fully seated
10. ☐ O2 cell date checked, cell and cable inspected or replaced, cell calibrated
11. ☐ Gas module water trap and sample line replaced; exhaust checked
12. ☐ Unidirectional valves, APL, bellows or piston inspected
13. ☐ Hoses, yokes, pin-index pins and seals, flowtubes inspected
14. ☐ Batteries checked against replacement interval; filters cleaned or replaced
15. ☐ Service calibrations and full checkout done; tag stays until verification passes

Download the record sheet: [CSV](https://serviceengineer.academy/guides/anaesthesia-machine-pm-checklist.csv). It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.

## Record on every sheet

- Asset number, serial number, model, software version and location
- Date, engineer, and the analyser or test tools used with their calibration dates
- For each step: pass, fail or not applicable; the measured value and the limit where there is one
- Parts replaced, with part numbers
- Decision: in service, in service with restriction, or out of service; next due date
