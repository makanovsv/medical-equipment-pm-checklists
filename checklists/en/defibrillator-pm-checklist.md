
# Defibrillator preventive maintenance checklist

This is the checklist we use in the course [Defibrillator and ECG Service — Level 1](https://serviceengineer.academy/course/defib-l1/). It lists what to check on a defibrillator during planned maintenance, in the order we teach it. Print it or download the CSV and fill in one sheet per visit.

**Use it with the manufacturer's service manual, not instead of it.** The manual sets the intervals, kit parts, test limits and calibration steps for your exact model and software. Where the manual is stricter, the manual wins.

## Checklist

1. ☐ Get the exact complaint, the mode and the event log before changing anything
2. ☐ If the device failed in clinical use: preserve it and the log, take it out of service, inform the responsible person
3. ☐ Test with known good battery, cable and pads into the analyser
4. ☐ Check configuration: mode at power-on, sync, energy defaults, filters, printing
5. ☐ Use the manufacturer's troubleshooting table for error codes
6. ☐ Escalate HV faults with full information; arrange a replacement unit
7. ☐ Before return: full performance verification, electrical safety, final self-test, clinical settings, in-date battery and pads
8. ☐ Record measured values, instruments, parts, result, next due date and label

Download the record sheet: [CSV](https://serviceengineer.academy/guides/defibrillator-pm-checklist.csv). It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.

## Record on every sheet

- Asset number, serial number, model, software version and location
- Date, engineer, and the analyser or test tools used with their calibration dates
- For each step: pass, fail or not applicable; the measured value and the limit where there is one
- Parts replaced, with part numbers
- Decision: in service, in service with restriction, or out of service; next due date
