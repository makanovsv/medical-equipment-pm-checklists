
# Electrosurgical unit preventive maintenance checklist

This is the checklist we use in the course [Electrosurgical Unit Service — Level 1](https://serviceengineer.academy/course/esu-l1/). It lists what to check on an electrosurgical unit during planned maintenance, in the order we teach it. Print it or download the CSV and fill in one sheet per visit.

**Use it with the manufacturer's service manual, not instead of it.** The manual sets the intervals, kit parts, test limits and calibration steps for your exact model and software. Where the manual is stricter, the manual wins.

## Checklist

1. ☐ Test the generator with known-good accessories before blaming it
2. ☐ Inspect every reusable accessory along its full length under good light
3. ☐ Check each footswitch pedal for correct mode and immediate release
4. ☐ Confirm single-use items are not reprocessed and pads are within expiry
5. ☐ Follow the manufacturer's PM interval or the local rule, whichever is stricter
6. ☐ Record generator software version, analyser identity, every value and limit, and removed items

Download the record sheet: [CSV](https://serviceengineer.academy/guides/electrosurgical-unit-pm-checklist.csv). It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.

## Record on every sheet

- Asset number, serial number, model, software version and location
- Date, engineer, and the analyser or test tools used with their calibration dates
- For each step: pass, fail or not applicable; the measured value and the limit where there is one
- Parts replaced, with part numbers
- Decision: in service, in service with restriction, or out of service; next due date
